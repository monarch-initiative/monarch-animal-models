Transparent by design

Two sources, kept apart

DisMech curation and Alliance member assertions are normalized into one record shape but never merged. Species and categories map only through documented exact-match tables; disease precision comes from MONDO subclass axioms; orthology comes from the KG and is labeled as derived. Missing values stay visible.
